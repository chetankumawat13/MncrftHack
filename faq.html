FAQ's

What is JEC HACK 1.O ?

JEC Hack 1.0 is a 36-hour hackathon where students and innovators create real-world tech solutions. It's a platform to learn, build, network, and compete for amazing prizes while working on projects that matter.

When and Where is Jec Hack 1.O taking place

JEC Hack 1.0 will be held on May 7-8th, 2026. The event location will be announced soon. Stay tuned to our social media channels for updates!

Who can Participate in Jec Hack 1.O ?

JEC Hack 1.0 is open to all students currently enrolled in any educational institution across India. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced developer, everyone is welcome!

How do I register for the Hackathon

Click the "Register Now" button on our homepage and fill out the registration form with your details. You'll receive a confirmation email once your registration is complete.
