ABOUT JEC HACK

JEC Hack is a 36-hour in-person innovation marathon where ideas turn into reality and students become creators. It is not just about writing code, but about building solutions that can create real impact in the world. At JEC Hack, passionate developers, designers, and problem-solvers come together under one roof to collaborate, experiment, and push their limits. The energy of working face-to-face, the pressure of time, and the thrill of creation make this experience truly unforgettable.
